# Global Job-Board Abyss

*Public job-board and recruiter-post specimens observed 2026-09-08.*

The old score could badly underrate narrow jobs because harmless dimensions averaged away catastrophic ones. **Horrendous Score v2** fixes that by letting the four worst burdens dominate. Time capture, physical danger, compensation, and dependency now hit the main score directly.

## Ceiling references

The job-board specimens are horrible; the global labor-market research gives the scale its ceiling.

| Reference case | Approximate v2 calibration |
|---|---:|
| Debt-bonded brick-kiln labor | **10.0** |
| Bangladesh shipbreaking | **~9.8–9.9** |
| Hazardous sewer / septic-tank entry | **~9.6–9.8** |

Those are research anchors rather than ordinary public-posting scores. They mark the point where low pay stacks with lethal exposure, extreme time demands, debt, or loss of exit freedom.

## Reading the cabinet

V2 asks how bad the **worst parts of the bargain** are. An employer no longer gets a scoring discount because a 12-hour, seven-day job happens to have a simple title.

The strongest pure schedule specimen in this batch is Chennai. The strongest danger-pay specimen is Dubai rope access. The strongest live-in pair is Mumbai construction security and Durban housekeeping. The Gulf cleaner posts dominate the sheer hours-to-cash ratio.
