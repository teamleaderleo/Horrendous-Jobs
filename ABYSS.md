## Ceiling references

The job-board specimens are horrible; the global labor-market research gives the scale its ceiling.

| Reference case | Approximate v2 calibration |
|---|---:|
| Debt-bonded brick-kiln labor | **10.0** |
| Bangladesh shipbreaking | **~9.8–9.9** |
| Hazardous sewer / septic-tank entry | **~9.6–9.8** |

Those are research anchors rather than ordinary public-posting scores. They mark the point where low pay stacks with lethal exposure, extreme time demands, debt, or loss of exit freedom.

## Reading the cabinet

V2 asks how bad the **worst parts of the bargain** are. An employer no longer gets a scoring discount because a 12-hour, seven-day job happens to have a simple title.

The strongest pure schedule specimens now include Chennai's 12×7 guard post and Bhadreswar's explicit 12×30 guard post. The strongest danger-pay specimen remains Dubai rope access. The strongest live-in pair remains Mumbai construction security and Durban housekeeping. The September 17 additions broaden the cabinet into chemical-tank cleaning, sludge work, recruitment-charge dependency, and another openly advertised 300+ hour monthly schedule.
