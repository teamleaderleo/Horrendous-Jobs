# Horrendous Score Rubric

The **Horrendous Score** runs from **0.0 to 10.0**. It measures the advertised effort-to-reward bargain in a job posting.

The score is editorial, but the evidence behind it should be concrete and sourced.

## Dimensions

Score each dimension from **0 to 5**.

| Dimension | 0 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Scope creep** | Narrow, coherent role | Several adjacent functions | Multiple professions hiding under one title |
| **Responsibility / authority gap** | Clear ownership and decision rights | Owns outcomes with partial control | Accountable for outcomes created by many people, with little visible authority |
| **Coordination burden** | Mostly individual work | Frequent cross-team follow-up | Human router: clients, vendors, executives, internal teams, calendars, approvals, trackers |
| **Urgency & interruption load** | Predictable work | Competing priorities | Live incidents, constant pivots, escalations, event-day or executive urgency |
| **Experience / credential tax** | Requirements fit scope and pay | Moderate inflation | Years of experience / specialized education for modest compensation and clerical-heavy work |
| **Emotional labor** | Little interpersonal load | Regular customer/stakeholder management | Constant service recovery, executive care, angry customers, fragile stakeholders, or concierge-level expectations |
| **After-hours exposure** | Stable scheduled hours | Occasional spillover | Evenings, weekends, travel, emergencies, on-call-like expectations |
| **Compensation penalty** | Strong pay/upside for the scope | Middling | Compensation looks strikingly weak against duties, location, requirements, and availability |

## Suggested calculation

Add the eight dimension scores for a raw total out of 40, then convert to 10:

```text
Horrendous Score = raw total / 4
```

Round to one decimal place.

Editors can move the final score by up to **±0.5** when the posting contains an unusually revealing combination the raw arithmetic misses. Explain any adjustment.

## Interpretation

| Score | Reading |
|---:|---|
| **0.0–3.9** | Ordinary employment bargain |
| **4.0–5.9** | Mildly cursed |
| **6.0–7.4** | Serious scope/pay friction |
| **7.5–8.4** | Horrendous |
| **8.5–9.4** | Elite employment horror |
| **9.5–10.0** | Museum-quality specimen |

## Evidence rules

- Record the **date observed**.
- Preserve the **currency and pay period** exactly enough to understand the offer.
- Distinguish advertised requirements from editorial interpretation.
- Summarize duties; keep direct excerpts short.
- Link to the source posting.
- If the listing disappears, keep the entry and mark the source inactive when known.
- Score the **posting**, not the people doing the job.

## Common score boosters

These phrases and patterns deserve attention because they often bundle several dimensions at once:

- “wear many hats”
- “no two days are the same”
- “fast-paced environment”
- “nothing falls through the cracks”
- “other duties as assigned”
- “anticipate needs before they arise”
- “multiple competing priorities”
- “occasional evenings and weekends”
- “serve as the key point of contact”
- “support the full lifecycle”
- “go-to resource”

None of these phrases proves a bad role by itself. The combination of duties, authority, availability, requirements, and pay earns the score.
