## The posting

A food-service supervisor role at CAD $17/hour responsible for supervising 5–10 people, hiring and training staff, creating schedules, handling complaints, maintaining stock/repair/sales/wastage records, preparing reports and order summaries, managing supplies, checking deliveries, and enforcing food/service quality. The work is also described as fast-paced, physically demanding, and requiring extended standing.

### Advertised responsibilities

- Supervise and coordinate 5–10 food-service workers.
- Train staff in duties, sanitation, and safety procedures.
- Hire food-service staff and establish work schedules.
- Requisition supplies and estimate meal-preparation requirements.
- Maintain quality standards and address customer complaints.
- Maintain stock, repair, sales, and wastage records.
- Prepare reports and food-order summaries.
- Supervise and check delivery of food trolleys.

### Advertised requirements

- Secondary-school graduation certificate.
- One year to less than two years of experience.
- Client focus, communication, flexibility, teamwork, and dependability.
- Ability to work in a fast-paced, physically demanding environment with extended standing.
