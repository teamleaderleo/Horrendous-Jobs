The listing is for construction-site security in Vasai East. It advertises a 12-hour 8 AM–8 PM shift, INR 15,000–20,000 monthly pay, one to two years of experience, and **living on construction client site** as a facility.

### Advertised responsibilities

- Maintain visitor logs.

- Monitor CCTV, alarms, and security equipment.
- Respond quickly to fires, disturbances, and medical emergencies.
- Inform senior staff during emergencies.

- One to two years of experience.
- Male candidates specified by the listing.
- Live on the construction client site.
