## The posting

A senior testing role responsible for leading the full User Acceptance Testing lifecycle for commercial-banking leasing programs. The analyst owns strategy, scenario generation, execution, defect governance, exit reporting and formal sign-off while coordinating business stakeholders, product owners, development teams, QA groups and onshore/offshore testing teams. Cognizant asks for six-plus years of professional software-testing experience plus recent banking-domain experience and lists a base salary of CAD $55,000–$73,000.

### Advertised responsibilities

- Lead UAT activities across complex commercial-banking and leasing programs.
- Create and execute UAT plans, scenarios and test cases.
- Coordinate business, product, development and QA stakeholders.
- Coordinate onshore/offshore testing teams.
- Track defects through resolution and drive defect triage.
- Execute regression testing after fixes and updates.
- Produce daily and weekly status reports.
- Prepare the official UAT exit report, walk results through leadership and secure formal sign-off.

### Advertised requirements

- 6+ years of professional software-testing experience, with strong UAT management and coordination.
- Recent banking-domain experience.
- Hands-on UAT planning, test-scenario generation, execution and exit reporting.
- Experience leading onshore/offshore teams and managing issues to closure.
- JIRA and ALM experience.
- Ability to produce executive sign-off presentations.
- Commercial-banking/leasing expertise, test automation and familiarity with agentic/prompt-engineering frameworks such as LangChain, AutoGPT or ReAct are listed as ways to stand out.
