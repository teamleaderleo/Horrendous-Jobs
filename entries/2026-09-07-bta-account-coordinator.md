## The posting

This role mixes agency account coordination with concierge-style executive support and event operations. The employee may be asked to source hard-to-get restaurant reservations, research venues and experiences, coordinate special client requests, manage gifting and transportation, prepare itineraries and guest lists, maintain trackers, chase supplier information, attend site visits, and work outside traditional hours when events or client requests demand it.

### Advertised responsibilities

- Support executive campaigns, hospitality programs, events, and special experiences.
- Source and coordinate restaurant reservations and premium hospitality options.
- Research restaurants, chefs, hotels, venues, cultural events, and experiences across Canada.
- Handle special client and executive requests when the answer is unclear.
- Coordinate with restaurants, hotels, venues, suppliers, and hospitality partners.
- Prepare itineraries, guest lists, briefing documents, schedules, invitations, and RSVP tracking.
- Support gifting, transportation, entertainment, and other experience requirements.
- Maintain files, trackers, timelines, quotes, invoices, and confirmations.
- Support Account Managers and senior staff with client requests and campaign deliverables.
- Attend site visits, restaurant meetings, and events.

### Advertised requirements / conditions

- 1–3 years of experience in account coordination, events, hospitality, marketing, restaurants, hotels, concierge services, or related work.
- Comfort working with clients, executives, venues, restaurants, and suppliers.
- Ability to manage changing priorities and multiple deadlines.
- Occasional work outside traditional office hours.
- Explicit “figure it out” mentality expected.
