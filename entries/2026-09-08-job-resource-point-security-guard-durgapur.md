## The posting

Job Resource Point advertises 25 immediate Security Guard vacancies for a plant in Muchipara/Durgapur. The job pays INR 15,000–17,000 per month with PF and ESI and states **12-hour duty**. The candidate's home is required to be at least 35 km away from Muchipara; free lodging and cooking equipment are provided.

### Advertised responsibilities

- Guard plant premises against theft, unauthorized entry, and security breaches.
- Verify employee, visitor, and vehicle entry/exit.
- Maintain entry registers and daily activity reports.
- Respond to suspicious activity, alarms, and emergencies.
- Follow security rules and procedures.

### Advertised requirements

- Minimum 10th pass; H.S. pass and above accepted.
- Height around 5'4"–5'5".
- Physically fit and able to stand or patrol.
- Able to read, write, and maintain basic documentation.
- Punctual, hardworking, and disciplined.
- Candidate's home should be at least 35 km from Muchipara.
