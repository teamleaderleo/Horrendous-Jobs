## The posting

This personal administrative assistant role combines calendar and meeting management, communications and gatekeeping, travel planning, documents and presentations, meeting follow-up, personal errands, event planning, vendor coordination, expense and invoice administration, budget records, research, and digital-tool troubleshooting. It asks for prior personal- or executive-assistant experience while advertising a starting rate of USD $4/hour.

### Advertised responsibilities

- Manage calendars, appointments, meetings, and priorities.
- Handle communications and act as a gatekeeper.
- Arrange travel and related logistics.
- Prepare documents, reports, presentations, filing, agendas, notes, and follow-up items.
- Complete personal errands and administrative requests.
- Plan events and coordinate vendors, timelines, and logistics.
- Track expenses, invoices, budgets, and financial records.
- Conduct research and prepare summaries.
- Use and troubleshoot digital productivity tools as needed.

### Advertised requirements

- Proven personal-assistant or executive-assistant experience.
- Strong organization, time management, communication, discretion, and problem-solving skills.
- Ability to handle shifting priorities and deadlines.
- Proficiency with common digital tools and a suitable remote-work setup.
