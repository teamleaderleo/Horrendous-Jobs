## The posting

A clinic office manager role that explicitly divides the week between clinical reception/administration and a health-food cafe. The employee supervises staff across clinic reception, administration, and cafe operations while also doing patient administration, insurance billing, vendor coordination, janitorial work, barista service, kitchen prep, dishwashing, sanitation, and physical lifting.

### Advertised responsibilities

- Manage patient flow, scheduling, waitlists, intake forms, payments, receipts, and insurance billing.
- Handle patient questions and concerns.
- Supervise front-of-house, administrative, and cafe staff, including scheduling and training.
- Maintain practitioner schedules, treatment-room assignments, facility bookings, patient records, SOPs, and operational reports.
- Order and track clinic and cafe supplies and manage vendors and deliveries.
- Reconcile daily tills and onboard new staff.
- Perform janitorial and organizing duties.
- Prepare and serve espresso drinks, tea, and other beverages; process cafe sales.
- Open and close the cafe, including cleaning, sanitizing, and equipment maintenance.
- Assist the chef with washing, chopping, portioning, labelling, plating, dishwashing, sanitation, and temperature logging.
- Lift boxes up to 50 lbs and perform manual cleaning and kitchen tasks.

### Advertised requirements

- Two or more years in clinic, medical-office, or dental-office administration, or comparable front-of-house management.
- Prior staff-supervision experience.
- Barista/cafe experience, including espresso preparation and milk steaming.
- Privacy awareness, food-safety knowledge, and strong customer service.
- Cooking experience and Food Handler Certification are listed as preferred.
