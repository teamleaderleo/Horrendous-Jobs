## The posting

The role supports a managing director across two law firms, a marketing company, and other related ventures. The employee is expected to manage executive communications and scheduling, chase tasks assigned across staff and outside parties, coordinate travel and events, run business and personal errands, track the executive's personal bills and accounts, and remain flexible around shifting priorities and occasional off-hours work.

### Advertised responsibilities

- Manage a heavily booked calendar across multiple businesses and triage email, calls, and WhatsApp Business accounts.
- Prepare the executive for meetings and maintain a live list of open items.
- Track tasks assigned to staff, outside counsel, and vendors; follow up until completion and escalate blockers.
- Take meeting notes, assign owners and deadlines, and circulate action items.
- Book travel, events, appearances, registrations, and deadlines.
- Coordinate personal and family scheduling alongside business commitments.
- Run court, registry, courier, bank, office-supply, client, office, and personal errands.
- Drive between offices, courts, and client meetings.
- Track personal accounts, bills, subscriptions, recurring payments, receipts, statements, and expense coding.
- Coordinate with the executive's bookkeeper, accountant, bank, and insurance contacts.
- Prepare documents and presentations and take on research, vendor comparisons, event planning, and process clean-up.

### Advertised requirements

- Strong organization, follow-through, writing, judgment, discretion, and numerical accuracy.
- Comfort with priorities changing throughout the day.
- Valid Ontario driver's licence and reliable access to a vehicle.
- Flexibility for occasional early starts, late finishes, and off-hours messages.
- Post-secondary education in a related field or equivalent practical experience.
- Executive-support experience is described as an asset.
- Background check and confidentiality agreement required.
