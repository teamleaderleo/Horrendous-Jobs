## The posting

CMiC calls this an entry-level Project Coordinator role, but the employee is expected to coordinate implementation resources and schedules, act as project lead for cloud customers, define scope with clients, assign internal tasks, analyze project risks, handle issues, communicate status to all participants, support change management, and travel to customer sites up to 25% of the time.

### Advertised responsibilities

- Coordinate project activities, resources, equipment, and information.
- Build project timeframes and schedules.
- Act as project lead for cloud-based customers.
- Liaise with clients to identify requirements, scope, and objectives.
- Assign tasks to internal teams and support schedule management.
- Create project documentation, plans, and reports.
- Track working hours, plans, and expenditures.
- Analyze risks and opportunities.
- Monitor progress and handle issues as they arise.
- Act as a point of contact and communicate project status to all participants.
- Ensure standards and requirements are met.
- Travel to customer sites up to 25% of the time.

### Advertised requirements

- One to three years of project coordination/support experience.
- University degree or equivalent experience.
- Ability to travel to the United States without restrictions.
- Strong consultative and relationship-building skills.
- Enterprise software implementation experience is an asset.
- Waterfall and agile methodology knowledge is an asset.
- Ability to support change-management processes across affected groups.
