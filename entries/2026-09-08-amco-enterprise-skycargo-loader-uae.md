## The posting

AMCO Enterprise's public UAE recruitment campaign listed multiple aviation-support, logistics, facilities, and security roles for Bangladeshi candidates. **SkyCargo Loader** was advertised at **AED 1,008/month**. The campaign also listed loaders at AED 1,105, warehouse workers at AED 1,000, housekeepers at AED 900, and kitchen stewards at AED 900.

The campaign advertises free accommodation, company transportation, free medical facilities, a renewable employment contract, and benefits under UAE labour law.

### Advertised responsibilities

The campaign names SkyCargo Loader as the position but does not give a detailed task list.

### Advertised requirements

- SSC or higher qualification.
- Good English communication.
- Age 21–38.
- Physically fit and well groomed.
- Relevant experience preferred.
- Recruitment targeted at Bangladeshi candidates through a Dhaka interview campaign.
