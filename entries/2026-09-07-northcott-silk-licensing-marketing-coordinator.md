## The posting

The role combines licensing compliance, packaging, content planning, influencer work, media coordination, photography support, events, trade shows, social content, approval workflows, cross-functional coordination, and analytics. It asks for three or more years of relevant experience while advertising CAD $42,000–$47,000.

### Advertised responsibilities

- Coordinate marketing execution for licensed fabric collections and maintain compliance with licensor guidelines.
- Manage approval submissions for marketing assets, packaging, and promotional content.
- Contribute to packaged-product development from concept through production.
- Plan content calendars across digital, print, and social channels for B2B and consumer audiences.
- Support photography and creative asset production.
- Develop influencer and brand-ambassador relationships.
- Coordinate media opportunities and collaborations.
- Manage timelines and logistics for materials, photoshoots, and licensor check-ins.
- Support trade shows, conventions, product launches, and consumer events.
- Track deliverables across multiple concurrent brand programs.
- Use analytics to improve marketing performance.

### Advertised requirements

- Three or more years of marketing, communications, or brand-development experience.
- Strong approval-workflow and deadline management.
- Written and visual communication skills with strong brand consistency.
- Familiarity with craft, fabric, quilting, or licensed consumer products.
- Understanding of both B2B and consumer marketing.
- Experience with influencer partnerships and cross-functional coordination.
- Comfort with digital-marketing tools, content-planning platforms, and social media.
