The role spans creator sourcing, campaign coordination, content review, communications, rate support, contract routing, onboarding, analytics, client reporting, payment processing, budget reconciliation, calendar maintenance, trend research, and cross-team coordination.

### Advertised responsibilities

- Identify and vet creators across TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube.
- Coordinate active campaigns, deliverables, content review, and timelines.
- Handle creator communications, rate discussions, contract routing, and onboarding.
- Pull performance data and contribute to client-facing reports and QBR materials.
- Process creator payments and reconcile campaign budgets.
- Maintain calendars, trackers, folders, and campaign-management sheets.

- 1–2 years of relevant creator, influencer, social-media, agency, or brand-side experience.
- Familiarity with major creator platforms and analytics tools.

- Ability to manage multiple workstreams and follow through on details.
- Bachelor's or post-secondary education in a related field, or equivalent professional experience.
