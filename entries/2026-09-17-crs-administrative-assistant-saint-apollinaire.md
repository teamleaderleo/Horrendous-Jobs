## The posting

An "Administrative Assistant" posting asking for a master's degree or equivalent experience and five years or more of experience, then assigning duties that include coordinating the HR department, directing and motivating staff, planning and controlling budgets, supervising and training workers, establishing policies, developing recruitment strategies, managing contracts, handling employee complaints, and recruiting and hiring. It also retains ordinary administrative work such as phones, appointments, email, inventory, correspondence, and office supplies.

### Advertised responsibilities

- Coordinate the HR department so it meets organizational goals.
- Direct, motivate, supervise, and train staff.
- Plan and control budgets and expenditures.
- Establish and implement policies and procedures.
- Develop recruitment strategies and recruit/hire staff.
- Manage contracts and respond to employee questions and complaints.
- Schedule appointments, answer phones and electronic enquiries, order supplies, maintain inventory, and proofread documents.

### Advertised requirements

- Master's degree or equivalent experience.
- Five years or more of experience.
- French working language.
- On-site work.
- Screening asks about shift/on-call availability.
- Scheduling categories include early morning, morning, day, evening, and night.
