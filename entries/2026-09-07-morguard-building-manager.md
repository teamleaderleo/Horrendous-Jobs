## The posting

A building-management role combining maintenance, contractor supervision, safety and security, tenant collections and disputes, legal notices, reporting, budgeting, leasing, staff supervision, and emergency response. The posting lists an on-call schedule and a minimum of five years of relevant experience.

### Advertised responsibilities

- Oversee and sometimes perform building repairs and maintenance.
- Coordinate suite turnovers, cleaning, inspections, contractors, and third-party quotes.
- Inspect for hazards, security issues, emergency readiness, environmental incidents, and health-and-safety compliance.
- Respond to emergencies including fire, flood, power failures, alarms, and environmental issues.
- Process invoices, purchase-order matching, and documentation.
- Address complex tenant issues involving collections, abatements, lease terms, and improvements.
- At some properties, issue and serve legal notices and attend tribunal hearings.
- Prepare weekly, monthly, and quarterly reporting and assist with annual budgets.
- At some properties, show and rent suites, complete checks, issue leases, and approve or disapprove applications.
- Oversee, train, and monitor site staff.

### Advertised requirements

- Minimum five years of relevant work experience or equivalent.
- Working knowledge of the Residential Tenancies Act and city bylaws.
- Strong negotiation, communication, team-building, and management skills.
- Possible after-hours emergency callouts with immediate response expected.
