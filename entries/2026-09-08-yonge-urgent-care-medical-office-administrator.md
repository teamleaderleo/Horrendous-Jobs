## The posting

This seven-day urgent-care role combines reception, patient registration, appointment and waiting-room flow, urgent-case prioritization, physician support, EMR documentation, referrals, imaging and lab coordination, OHIP billing and reconciliation, recalls, payments, supply administration, and some clinical support when trained. Evening and weekend rotation is part of the offer.

### Advertised responsibilities

- Register patients and manage phones, email, walk-ins, bookings, cancellations, and waiting-room flow.
- Coordinate patient movement and help prioritize urgent cases.
- Liaise with pharmacies, laboratories, imaging providers, and other outside services.
- Maintain EMR records, chart documentation, referrals, imaging requests, and physician forms.
- Support OHIP billing, reconciliation, payments, reports, and administrative records.
- Track recalls, tests, and follow-up items.
- Support patient onboarding, supplies, staff meetings, and clinic operations.
- When trained, assist with tasks such as vital signs, exam-room preparation, and vaccination workflow.

### Advertised requirements

- One to two years of medical-clinic experience.
- Experience with EMR systems and knowledge of PHIPA requirements.
- Ability to multitask in a busy environment, prioritize, and remain composed under pressure.
- Formal medical-office training is preferred.
- Availability for evening and weekend rotating shifts.
