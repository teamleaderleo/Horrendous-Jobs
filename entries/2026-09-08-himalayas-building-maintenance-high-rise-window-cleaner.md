## The posting

The role performs exterior window and glass cleaning on high-rise buildings throughout the GTA. Workers use PPE, fall-protection equipment, and high-rise safety systems while doing physically demanding outdoor work. Previous high-rise window-cleaning experience is preferred but not required; the employer says suitable candidates can be trained. The schedule is primarily Monday to Friday with occasional weekend work depending on project requirements and conditions.

### Advertised responsibilities

- Perform exterior window and glass cleaning on high-rise buildings.
- Assist with high-rise cleaning operations using appropriate equipment and safety systems.
- Follow company and job-site safety procedures.
- Properly use PPE and fall-protection equipment.
- Work with experienced crew members and supervisors.
- Travel to job sites throughout the GTA.

### Advertised requirements

- Comfort working at heights.
- Ability and willingness to learn high-rise window-cleaning techniques.
- Safety consciousness and ability to follow instructions.
- Physical capacity for demanding outdoor work.
- Reliability and ability to work as part of a crew.
- Previous high-rise or construction experience is an asset rather than a requirement.
- Working at Heights, WHMIS, and related certifications are listed as assets; required training must be completed before applicable high-rise work.
