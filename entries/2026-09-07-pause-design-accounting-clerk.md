## The posting

A part-time accounting-clerk role asking for 2–3 years of full-cycle accounting/bookkeeping experience, GAAP knowledge, QuickBooks proficiency, and responsibility across AP, reporting, general ledger work, payroll, government remittances, year-end preparation, and audit support.

### Advertised responsibilities

- Process and monitor accounts payable and supplier/trade-vendor invoices.
- Prepare weekly project reports, monthly financial statements, and ad-hoc analyses.
- Perform bank/account reconciliations and reconcile card and online-payment receipts.
- Maintain the general ledger and prepare year-end journal entries, accruals, and reconciliations.
- Process semi-monthly payroll, deductions, adjustments, overtime, vacation pay, payroll journal entries, ROEs, and year-end payroll filings.
- Work with the external accountant on HST/GST remittances, corporate-tax-return preparation, and audit support.

### Advertised requirements

- 2–3 years of full-cycle accounting or bookkeeping experience.
- Strong knowledge of GAAP and standard accounting practices.
- Full proficiency in QuickBooks Online.
- Post-secondary education is preferred.
