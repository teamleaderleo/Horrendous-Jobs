## The posting

This role spans financial reporting, budgeting, forecasting, cash-flow analysis, funder compliance, full-cycle bookkeeping, AP, AR, bank payments, invoicing, payroll administration, HR files, employee-cycle support, and general administrative work. The posting also requires travel plus flexibility for evenings, weekends, and overnight work.

### Advertised responsibilities

- Support monthly financial statements, budget variance analysis, annual budget and forecast work.
- Support cash-flow reporting and year-end financial review.
- Interpret funding agreements and prepare multi-program funder reporting and compliance documentation.
- Assist with the full accounting cycle in QuickBooks Online.
- Handle AP/AR reports, invoicing, bank payments, deposits, reconciliations, and vendor records.
- Prepare payroll packages, review timesheets, process honoraria and expenses, and assist with payroll compliance.
- Maintain HR files and support employee management-cycle processes.
- Provide additional operational and administrative support as requested.

### Advertised requirements / conditions

- Degree or diploma in Accounting, Finance, Business Administration, or equivalent experience.
- Minimum three years of accounting and payroll-processing experience preferred in a nonprofit setting.
- Strong knowledge of accounting principles and QuickBooks Online.
- Travel required.
- Flexible schedule including evenings, weekends, and overnights.
