## The posting

This HR Coordinator role spans recruitment, onboarding, offboarding, employment paperwork, HRIS maintenance, time and attendance, organizational charts, internal announcements, compliance training, Finance/Operations/HR reporting, SOP development, benefits and pension administration, audit support, corporate events, and general office projects. It also requires travel between company locations and occasional evening/weekend availability.

### Advertised responsibilities

- Post jobs, screen resumes, coordinate interviews, communicate with candidates, and conduct reference/background checks.
- Facilitate onboarding and employee lifecycle paperwork.
- Maintain ADP WorkforceNow records, employee files, time and attendance, and organizational charts.
- Prepare internal employment announcements.
- Facilitate and track compliance training and policy acknowledgements.
- Support reporting for Finance, Operations, and HR.
- Support SOP development.
- Support benefits and pension enrolments and changes.
- Support HR audit documentation requests.
- Support corporate event planning.
- Take on general office duties and assigned projects.

### Advertised requirements / conditions

- Post-secondary education in Human Resources or a similar field.
- 1–3 years of HR experience.
- Understanding of Ontario employment and occupational health and safety legislation.
- Travel between Toronto-area locations as required.
- Flexible schedule with occasional evenings and weekends.
