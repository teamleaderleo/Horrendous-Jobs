## The posting

The employer explicitly describes this as a “Jack/Jill of all trades coordinator/management hybrid.” The role is advertised at 15–20 hours per week but includes on-call reception across three to four consecutive days, a fixed weekly onsite shift, occasional urgent onsite matters, vacation coverage, and a two-year commitment.

### Advertised responsibilities

- Handle on-call reception, return messages quickly, and liaise with stakeholders.
- Support QuickBooks, accounts payable and accounts receivable.
- Troubleshoot clinic technology and work across scheduling, Google Workspace, design and office software.
- Perform weekly cleaning in addition to the clinic's cleaning service.
- Handle beginner maintenance tasks and equipment safety checks.
- Purchase, source, transport and receive clinic supplies.
- Supervise staff, vendors and tradespeople.
- Support onboarding and offboarding.
- Communicate with the landlord and other tenants and handle miscellaneous access tasks such as cutting keys.
- Cover absent support staff and assist the clinic owner with ad-hoc projects.

### Advertised requirements

- On-call availability for three to four consecutive days per week.
- Every Thursday onsite from 7:30 AM to 2:00 PM.
- Must live within roughly 30 minutes of Bloor/Yonge because urgent onsite matters can arise.
- Independent work, quick turnaround, advanced English communication, tech proficiency and basic maintenance skills.
- Two-year contract commitment.
