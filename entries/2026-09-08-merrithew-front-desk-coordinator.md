## The posting

The role combines front-desk reception, customer service, membership sales, lead follow-up, event support, payment handling, opening and closing, schedule coordination, cleaning, and social-media capture. The posting adds explicit sales goals, two or more years of experience, and availability for early mornings, evenings, and weekends.

### Advertised responsibilities

- Greet members and guests, manage check-ins, bookings, calls, email, payments, tours, and general inquiries.
- Sell memberships, packages, promotions, and retail products while following up with leads by phone, email, and text.
- Work toward monthly and quarterly sales goals and support referral initiatives.
- Help plan and execute in-studio and off-site events.
- Maintain membership records, schedules, POS transactions, and daily sales reconciliation.
- Perform opening and closing duties and coordinate with instructors, management, and other front-desk staff.
- Keep the front desk, changerooms, washrooms, studios, and equipment clean and stocked.
- Capture photo and video content for social media when needed.

### Advertised requirements

- Two or more years of front-desk, customer-service, or sales experience.
- Sales aptitude, including objection handling and converting prospects.
- Ability to multitask under pressure and de-escalate sensitive issues.
- Availability for early-morning, evening, and weekend shifts.
