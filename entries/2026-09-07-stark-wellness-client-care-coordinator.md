## The posting

A psychotherapy-clinic front-desk role paying from $20/hour that asks for broad weekday, evening and weekend availability, two to three years of client-care/customer-service experience, and a preference for psychology undergraduate students or graduates. The role handles booking, payments, intake/consent administration, client communication and quality-assurance follow-up.

### Advertised responsibilities

- Support psychotherapists with bookings, payments and intake/consent forms.
- Welcome clients and handle phone/email support.
- Process payments at the end of sessions.
- Follow up on payments and securely manage payment information and documents.
- Maintain scheduling software, provider schedules and treatment offerings.
- Communicate patiently and professionally with challenging clients.
- Manage multiple simultaneous projects and deadlines.

### Advertised requirements

- Must live in Toronto within roughly 30 minutes of the office.
- Must be available weekdays, evenings and weekends.
- Two to three years of client-care/customer-service experience preferred, ideally in a medical or therapy office.
- Psychology undergraduate students and graduates are preferred/welcome.
- Strong comfort with Microsoft Office, Google Drive, Gmail, Dropbox, Mac and Windows.
- Application asks candidates to submit a personality-test result in addition to application materials.
