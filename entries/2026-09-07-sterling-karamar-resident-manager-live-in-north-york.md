## The posting

A live-in resident manager responsible for the building's physical condition, repairs, cleaning, leasing, rent collection, resident service, safety checks, contractors, inventory, and emergency response. The posting requires participation in an on-call rotation with a maximum 15-minute emergency response time.

### Advertised responsibilities

- Supervise trades and suppliers and coordinate facilities service and repairs.
- Investigate maintenance requests and perform minor repairs.
- Supervise or assist with cleaning, snow removal, landscaping, garbage handling, and appliance movement.
- Clean common areas, laundry rooms, washrooms, staircases, elevators, garbage areas, parking areas, and exterior grounds.
- Communicate with tenants, deliver notices, answer questions, and support tenant retention.
- Show vacant suites, maintain guest/traffic records, process leasing steps, and perform move-in inspections.
- Follow up on rent arrears by phone, email, and door knocking.
- Inspect safety systems, mechanical rooms, sidewalks, and driveways and maintain safety logs.
- Participate in an on-call rotation and respond to emergencies within 15 minutes.

### Advertised requirements

- High school diploma.
- Three to five years of technical and practical experience preferred.
- Previous resident-manager experience preferred.
- Ability to lift up to 50 lb and work indoors and outdoors in varied conditions.
- High tolerance for stress, initiative, planning, customer service, and sound judgment.
