## The posting

The role combines catering sales, business development, client relationship management, event planning, staffing, rentals, venue sourcing, bar operations, proposals, contracts, and live event execution. The posting says the employee may move between weddings, brand activations, and unusual production environments while working flexible evenings and weekends.

### Advertised responsibilities

- Develop strategies to grow catering sales and expand the client base.
- Work directly with clients on menus, décor, bar operations, venue sourcing, and event planning.
- Coordinate with culinary and operations teams on customized catering programs.
- Manage event scheduling, staff bookings, and rental bookings.
- Prepare proposals, contracts, and detailed event plans.
- Generate new business and drive revenue.
- Manage and grow client and venue relationships through event completion.
- Execute events in a wide range of locations and operating conditions.

### Advertised requirements

- About three years of catering/event-planning experience preferred.
- Ability to work in an intense environment and manage multiple projects simultaneously.
- Strong problem solving under pressure and relationship management.
- Knowledge of the Toronto event market.
- Competence with office software, Adobe InDesign, and event-management software.
- Flexible hours, including evenings and weekends as needed.
- Smart Serve and driver's licence are preferred.
