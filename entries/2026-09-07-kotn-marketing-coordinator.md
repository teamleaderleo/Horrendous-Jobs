## The posting

This marketing coordinator role spans integrated campaign coordination, cross-functional project tracking, creator management, content shoots, retail and cultural event execution, vendor logistics, reporting, analytics, and brand stewardship. The posting also asks the employee to work occasional evenings and weekends, travel as needed, and explicitly be willing to stretch beyond the role's scope.

### Advertised responsibilities

- Manage the marketing calendar across launches, campaigns, and key moments.
- Keep merchandising, retail, ecommerce, growth, creative, and brand teams aligned through timelines and tracked deliverables.
- Maintain central documentation, trackers, and marketing resources.
- Act as a central communication point across teams.
- Source and manage user-generated-content creators and coordinate content shoots.
- Coordinate retail activations, community events, cultural programming, and in-store events.
- Manage event communications, vendors, supplies, setup, and on-the-ground execution.
- Pull, organize, and synthesize marketing performance data.
- Maintain reporting cadences and translate analytics into actionable insights.

### Advertised requirements

- One to two years of relevant marketing experience.
- Comfort with Google Analytics, Shopify, campaign dashboards, and multiple concurrent workstreams.
- Strong cross-functional communication and organization.
- Occasional evenings and weekends for events.
- Travel as needed; driver's licence is an asset.
- Willingness to create missing processes and support work beyond the defined scope.
