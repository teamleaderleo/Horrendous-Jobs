## The posting

A part-time administrative assistant is described as the outgoing patient liaison responsible for day-to-day clinic operation. The role requires availability from open to close across Monday through Sunday, including weekends, and pays from $18/hour.

### Advertised responsibilities

- Reception, visitor/patient screening and telephone handling.
- Educate patients on available treatments.
- Liaise with referring physician offices and chase required referral documentation.
- Book appointments, tests and procedures and explain preparation details.
- Maintain electronic and paper filing systems.
- Coordinate mail/courier work and office-equipment maintenance.
- Process private-treatment and insurance payments.
- Perform other duties as assigned.

### Advertised requirements

- Availability Monday through Sunday, open to close; weekend availability required.
- Strong customer service, communication, organization and judgment.
- EMR experience is an asset.
- Sales experience is an asset.
- Backgrounds in performing arts/theatre, design, media creation and business social-media management are also listed as assets.
