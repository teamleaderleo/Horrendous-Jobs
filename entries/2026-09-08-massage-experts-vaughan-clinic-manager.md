## The posting

The Clinic Manager is expected to oversee day-to-day operations while also working the front desk. The role spans patient service, bookings and payments, membership and retail sales, practitioner scheduling, staff hiring and coaching, complaint handling, performance metrics, revenue growth, inventory, opening and closing, and clinic cleanliness.

### Advertised responsibilities

- Oversee daily clinic operations while actively working front-desk shifts.
- Handle calls, bookings, cancellations, payments, memberships, gift cards, rebooking, and patient questions.
- Promote services, retail products, and memberships.
- Coordinate therapist schedules and practitioner coverage.
- Hire, onboard, train, schedule, coach, and manage front-desk staff performance.
- Resolve patient complaints and service issues.
- Track bookings, rebooking, member enrollment, retention, cancellations, and revenue.
- Identify opportunities to improve clinic growth and operating processes.
- Maintain inventory, complete opening and closing duties, and keep the clinic clean.
- Communicate across the owner, staff, practitioners, patients, and outside partners.

### Advertised requirements

- Two or more years of relevant experience.
- Supervisory or management experience strongly preferred.
- Ability to hold a team accountable and handle difficult customer situations.
- Willingness to work the front desk while managing clinic operations.
- Evening and weekend availability as required.
