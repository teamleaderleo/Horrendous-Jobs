## The posting

A full-time medical/admin assistant role that spans patient scheduling, physician/nurse support, quality assurance, cross-training, procedure assistance, equipment operation after training, inventory, maintenance, cleaning/compliance documentation and client relations. The required availability window covers Monday through Sunday from 6:00 AM to 5:30 PM, with two rotating weekends per month.

### Advertised responsibilities

- Support physicians, nurses and management with patient care, scheduling and administrative work.
- Complete ongoing mandated training and attend clinic conferences, events and meetings.
- Support quality-assurance tasks and privacy-law compliance.
- Cross-train for other clinic roles.
- Assist physicians and nurses with procedures.
- Operate equipment after training, potentially including x-ray fluoroscopy, ultrasound and infusion pumps.
- Manage inventory and supplies and identify maintenance/repair needs.
- Help ensure cleaning, staff compliance, dosimetry, equipment maintenance and documentation meet clinic protocols.
- Support relationships with referring physicians, patients and external organizations.
- Monitor quality-assurance protocols and maintain Basic Life Support certification.

### Advertised requirements

- Full-time availability Monday–Sunday between 6:00 AM and 5:30 PM.
- Five eight-hour shifts per week, including two weekends per month.
- Strong computer, data-entry, customer-service and phone skills.
- Medical-office experience preferred.
