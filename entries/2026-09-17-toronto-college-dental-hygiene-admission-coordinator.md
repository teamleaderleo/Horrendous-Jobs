## The posting

A full-time admissions role serving as the first contact between applicants and a private dental-hygiene college. The coordinator administers and marks admissions examinations, conducts pre-admission interviews, evaluates transcripts, prepares admission documents and acceptance packages, assesses enrolment contracts and submitted documents, delivers orientations, and handles enrolment letters and Student Aid. The posting body says a bachelor's degree is required and advertises CAD $45,000–$48,000.

### Advertised responsibilities

- Serve as the first point of contact between applicants and the college.
- Administer the admissions examination and pre-admission interview.
- Mark pre-admission examinations.
- Evaluate applicant transcripts.
- Prepare admission documents and manage admission processes.
- Deliver applicant orientation.
- Send conditional-acceptance packages and student-enrolment contracts.
- Assess student contracts and supporting documents.
- Handle enrolment letters and Student Aid matters.
