## The posting

A junior software-programmer role supporting design and implementation of Canadian Department of National Defence Command and Control specialty applications. The posting requires five days in office, a valid Secret Level II security clearance, a college diploma in engineering/computer technology, and at least two years with Java, C++, or C#, while paying CAD $45,000–$54,000/year.

### Advertised responsibilities

- Translate detailed flow charts into coded machine instructions and work with technical personnel on program planning.
- Design methods and procedures for small systems and subsystems of larger systems.
- Select and incorporate available software programs.
- Prepare sample data and perform system-acceptance testing.
- Correct program errors and modify operational sequences.
- Produce specifications, flow charts, diagrams, layouts, programming and operating instructions.
- Create and modify code, screens, reports, forms, manuals, data files, and procedures.
- Support reliability and scalability of DND Command and Control specialty applications.

### Advertised requirements

- Valid Secret Level II security clearance.
- College diploma in an engineering or computer-technology field; bachelor's degree preferred.
- At least two years of Java, C++, or C# experience.
- Experience with high-level languages, CASE tools, SAFe or another mandated development process, and integrated product teams.
- Problem-solving, troubleshooting, communication, and collaboration skills.
