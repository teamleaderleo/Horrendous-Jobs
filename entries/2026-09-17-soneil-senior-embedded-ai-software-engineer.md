## The posting

A senior embedded/AI role for EV charging and energy-management products. The job spans embedded firmware, hardware/software integration, electrical monitoring, communications protocols, AI/ML forecasting and predictive control, prototyping, lab and field testing, validation, deployment, and multidisciplinary engineering collaboration. It requires eight-plus years of embedded software experience and a broad stack across C/C++/Python, Embedded Linux/RTOS, microcontrollers, buses/protocols, debugging tools, and ML libraries.

### Advertised responsibilities

- Design, develop, test, and maintain embedded software for EV charging and energy-management systems.
- Integrate firmware across embedded controllers, electrical-monitoring devices, communication interfaces, and charging hardware.
- Develop and integrate AI/ML forecasting and predictive-control capabilities.
- Troubleshoot hardware/software, communications, and system-integration issues.
- Participate in research, prototyping, laboratory and field testing, validation, and product deployment.
- Evaluate new technologies and improve embedded, EV-charging, and intelligent energy-management solutions.
- Own assigned development activities and collaborate across engineering disciplines.

### Advertised requirements

- Bachelor's or master's degree in Computer Science, Electrical/Computer Engineering, Electronics Engineering, or related field.
- Eight-plus years of embedded software-development experience.
- Strong C, C++, and Python.
- Embedded Linux and RTOS experience.
- ARM, STM32, NXP, ESP32, AVR, PIC, Arduino, and Raspberry Pi experience.
- MODBUS, SPI, I2C, UART, CAN, RS-485, TCP/IP, and UDP knowledge.
- JTAG, GDB, oscilloscope, logic-analyzer, and serial-protocol-analyzer experience.
- AI/ML, forecasting, predictive modeling, TensorFlow/Keras, scikit-learn, and embedded/IoT integration experience.
- Complete product-lifecycle experience from concept and prototype through testing and implementation.
- EV charging, cloud-connected IoT, mobile development, SQL, JavaScript/Node.js, C#, HTML/CSS, and commercialization experience are listed as desired skills.
