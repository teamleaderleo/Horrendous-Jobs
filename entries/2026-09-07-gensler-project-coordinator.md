## The posting

The coordinator supports design and project managers from proposal through close-out, with contract administration, legal-comment routing, staffing and revenue plans, schedules, data management, billings, project metrics, and internal follow-up. The listing asks for an architecture-, architectural-technology-, or interior-design-related bachelor's degree and 4–8 years of experience.

### Advertised responsibilities

- Write, edit, administer, and track project agreements and authorizations.
- Liaise between in-house counsel, design managers, and project accountants on contract comments and fees.
- Enter staffing and revenue plans and create project schedules.
- Use project-management and data tools to identify areas for improvement.
- Help the studio understand and hit firmwide staffing, revenue, contract, and related project metrics.
- Support architectural billings and project close-out work.

### Advertised requirements

- Bachelor's degree in Architecture, Architectural Technology, or Interior Design.
- 4–8 years of experience.
- Microsoft Office proficiency and strong written and verbal communication.
- Legal or contract-review experience is treated as an asset.
